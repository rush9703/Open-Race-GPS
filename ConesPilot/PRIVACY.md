# ConesPilot Privacy Policy

*Last updated: February 11, 2026*

## Overview

ConesPilot ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is used.

## Data Collection

**ConesPilot does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

### Camera Access
The App uses your device's camera exclusively for Augmented Reality (AR) functionality. Camera data is processed locally on your device in real-time and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

### Location Access (Optional)
If granted permission, the App may access your device's location to provide contextual information. Location data is processed locally and is never shared with third parties.

## Data Storage

All course configurations and app preferences are stored locally on your device. No data is uploaded to external servers.

## Third-Party Services

ConesPilot does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

ConesPilot does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
