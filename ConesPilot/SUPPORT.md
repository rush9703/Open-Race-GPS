# ConesPilot Support

## About ConesPilot

ConesPilot is an augmented reality (AR) cone and marker placement app for iPhone and iPad. It helps athletes, coaches, and enthusiasts design and lay out training courses with precision — no tape measures or guesswork required.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What devices are supported?
ConesPilot works on all iPhones and iPads running iOS 16 or later. Devices with LiDAR (iPhone 12 Pro and later, iPad Pro 2020 and later) get enhanced depth-based placement accuracy.

### Does the app require an internet connection?
No. ConesPilot works entirely offline. Course design, AR placement, and saving/loading configurations all work without an internet connection.

### What sports is this app designed for?
ConesPilot is versatile and can be used for:
- **Inline/roller skating** — speed slalom, freestyle slalom
- **Agility training** — football, soccer, basketball drills
- **Motorsport** — gymkhana, autocross cone layouts
- **Any discipline** that uses cones or markers for training

### How accurate is the AR placement?
On LiDAR-equipped devices, placement guidance is accurate to within a few centimeters. On non-LiDAR devices, accuracy depends on lighting conditions and surface texture — flat, well-lit surfaces with visual features produce the best results.

### What cone types are available?
- **Standard Cone** — 20cm radius, 50cm height (traffic cone)
- **Small Marker** — 10cm radius, 8cm height (disc marker)
- **Skating Cone** — 5cm radius, 15cm height (slalom pylon)
- **No Cones** — Guide wires and cross markers only, for minimal visual setup

### Can I save and share my course layouts?
Yes. Courses can be saved within the app and exported as JSON files for sharing with teammates or coaches. Import shared courses with a single tap.

### What languages are supported?
ConesPilot is available in 11 languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, German, Korean, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Hindi, and Arabic.

## Troubleshooting

### AR tracking is unstable or drifting
- Ensure adequate lighting — avoid dimly lit or dark environments
- Point the camera at surfaces with visual texture (avoid blank walls or uniform floors)
- Move slowly and steadily when scanning
- If the device is overheating, pause and let it cool down

### Cones are floating above/below the ground
- Walk the camera closer to the ground surface before placing the origin
- On LiDAR devices, check that the depth confidence indicator shows green
- Ensure the ground surface is flat and not reflective

### The app crashes or freezes
- Force quit and relaunch the app
- Ensure your device is running the latest iOS version
- Free up storage space if your device is nearly full
- If the issue persists, please contact us (see below)
